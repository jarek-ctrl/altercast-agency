The AlterCast platform

AlterCast is a legal “Airbnb for faces”

Brands increasingly create ads with AI. Instead of generating anonymous faces without anyone's consent, they license the likenesses of real, verified people — on clearly defined terms.

You don't sell your face. You grant a licence — for one campaign, for a limited time, within your rules.
Learn more at altercast.ai

A licence, not a sale

Your likeness stays yours. A brand buys the right to use it in one campaign — for a set time, territory and channels (print, TV, digital).

You set the rules

You choose the industries and contexts you're happy to appear in. No need to approve every ad — everything must stay within your settings.

Verified people only

Every profile belongs to a real person who has passed identity verification. That protects you from impersonation and brands from legal risk.

Full transparency

Every month you get a report: who licensed your likeness, for which campaign, for how long and for how much.

Payouts without Stripe

Stripe doesn't pay out in your country? We'll pay you.

AlterCast pays talent through Stripe Connect. These payouts are only available to people in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, the US and Canada. If you live anywhere else, you can't set up payouts yourself — but you can still earn through the agency.

We review every application individually and make payouts in line with EU regulations. Not sure whether your country qualifies? Fill in the form and we'll check.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is applying through the agency different from registering on AlterCast myself?

You can register on AlterCast yourself if you live in a country where Stripe supports payouts. Through the agency, you hand everything over to us: setting up your profile, contexts and licences, plus all settlements. If you live in a country without Stripe payouts, the agency still lets you earn on AlterCast.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Registration, verification and your portfolio are free. The agency keeps 25% of its net revenue from your licences — we only earn when you do.

When and how do I get paid?

We settle monthly, based on the platform's reports, and transfer the money to the bank account you provide in the form (IBAN, plus SWIFT/BIC for foreign accounts).

Do I have to approve every ad?

No. You set your rules up front — industries, contexts and exclusivity — and every licence must comply with them.

Will my photos be used to train AI?

No. Your photos are used for identity verification and to create your portfolio. Originals are never shared with brands.

What photos do I need?

Three clear photos: front-facing, at a 45° angle and in profile. You can upload them in the form, send a link or arrange a photo shoot with the AlterCast team.

Do I need to create an account to apply?

No. You fill in the form directly on this site, with no sign-in, and you can upload your photos right in the form.

Can I opt out?

Yes. The agreement can be terminated with one month's notice. We then remove your profile; licences sold earlier stay valid until they expire.

Can I contract as a company?

Yes. In the form you choose whether you're licensing your likeness as a private individual or through a company.

Who runs AlterCast Agency?

AlterCast Agency is run by APTIV sp. z o.o., based in Gdynia, Poland — a likeness rights management agency working with the AlterCast platform.
